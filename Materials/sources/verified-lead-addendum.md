# Lead editor addendum: gaps closed after the dossiers

Verified by the lead editor on 2026-10-23 (pages opened).

## Gut serotonin does not cross the blood-brain barrier (Chapter 5)
- Bektaş A, Erdal H, Ulusoy M, Uzbay IT. (2020). Does seratonin in the intestines make you happy? *Turkish Journal of Gastroenterology*, 31(10), 721-723. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33169710/) / [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7659911/). States gut serotonin cannot reach the brain because it cannot cross the blood-brain barrier.
- Cai Y, Li X, Zhou H, Zhou J. (2022). The serotonergic system dysfunction in diabetes mellitus. *Frontiers in Cellular Neuroscience*, 16, 899069. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fncel.2022.899069). States 5-HT cannot physically cross the BBB, so peripheral and central serotonin systems are functionally separate.
- Jenkins TA, et al. (2016). Influence of tryptophan and serotonin on mood and cognition with a possible role of the gut-brain axis. *Nutrients*, 8(1), 56. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4728667/). Use for: tryptophan must cross the BBB for brain serotonin synthesis. (It does NOT itself state the serotonin BBB fact; don't cite it for that.)
- Canonical phrasing: "Serotonin made in the gut does not cross the blood-brain barrier. The brain makes its own serotonin from tryptophan, an amino acid that can cross."

## Food allergy vs food intolerance (Chapter 12)
- NIAID-Sponsored Expert Panel (Boyce JA, et al.). (2010). Guidelines for the diagnosis and management of food allergy in the United States. *J Allergy Clin Immunol*, 126(6 Suppl), S1-58. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21134576/) / [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4241964/). Defines food allergy as an adverse effect from a specific, reproducible immune response; most food allergy is IgE-mediated but non-IgE forms exist; non-immune reactions are food intolerances; lactose intolerance is the example (not immune-based).
- NIDDK, Definition & Facts for Celiac Disease. https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/celiac-disease/definition-facts . Celiac is different from wheat allergy; about 2 million people in the US have celiac disease; about 1% worldwide.
- Tang J, Krushelnycky L, Shaqo A, Cho CE. (2025). A comprehensive review of nutritional influences on the serotonergic system. *Advances in Nutrition*, 100524. [PMC12553067](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12553067/) (PMID 40998119). Full text states peripheral serotonin does not cross the BBB, has local effects such as GI motility, and can communicate with the brain via the vagus nerve; also that tryptophan must cross the BBB for brain serotonin synthesis. (Added 2026-10-23 as the strongest peer-reviewed source for the BBB fact.)
