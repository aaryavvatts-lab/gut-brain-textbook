# References: Medicine for the Gut-Brain Axis

1. [About C. diff](https://www.cdc.gov/c-diff/about/index.html) - Centers for Disease Control and Prevention - A plain-language overview of *C. difficile* infection: symptoms, who is at risk, and how antibiotics raise the risk. Source for the "almost half a million infections" figure in Section 17.2.

2. [REBYOTA (fecal microbiota, live-jslm)](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/vaccines/rebyota) - US Food and Drug Administration - The FDA's product page, with the exact approved use and links to the November 30, 2022 approval letter and review documents.

3. [FDA Approves First Orally Administered Fecal Microbiota Product for the Prevention of Recurrence of Clostridioides difficile Infection](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-orally-administered-fecal-microbiota-product-prevention-recurrence-clostridioides) - US Food and Drug Administration, April 26, 2023 - The Vowst announcement, including the trial results (12.4% vs 39.8% recurrence) and the risks of products made from human stool.

4. [Fecal microbiota transplantation for the treatment of recurrent Clostridioides difficile](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10125800/) - Minkoff NZ, et al. *Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews*, 2023 - A systematic review of 6 randomized trials showing FMT clears recurrent *C. diff* in adults with healthy immune systems. Its plain-language summary is a good model of honest evidence reporting.

5. [Fecal transplantation for treatment of inflammatory bowel disease](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37094824/) - Imdad A, et al. *Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews*, 2023 - The review behind Section 17.6's conclusion that FMT for ulcerative colitis is promising but low certainty, and very uncertain for Crohn's disease.

6. [Understanding Unapproved Use of Approved Drugs "Off Label"](https://www.fda.gov/patients/learn-about-expanded-access-and-other-treatment-options/understanding-unapproved-use-approved-drugs-label) - US Food and Drug Administration - Explains what FDA approval means, why doctors sometimes prescribe drugs off-label, and what questions patients can ask.

7. [Step 3: Clinical Research](https://www.fda.gov/patients/drug-development-process/step-3-clinical-research) - US Food and Drug Administration - The FDA's description of clinical trial phases 1 to 4, including how many people take part and what share of drugs move on to the next phase.

8. [Premarket Approval P970003/S050: VNS Therapy System for depression](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfPMA/pma.cfm?id=P970003S050) - US Food and Drug Administration device database - The official record of the July 15, 2005 approval of vagus nerve stimulation for treatment-resistant depression, with the exact indication. Links from it lead to the original 1997 epilepsy approval.

9. [Summary of Safety and Effectiveness Data: SetPoint System (PMA P240039)](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf24/P240039B.pdf) - US Food and Drug Administration, 2025 - The FDA's summary of the evidence behind the July 30, 2025 approval of vagus nerve stimulation for rheumatoid arthritis, including the RESET-RA trial results. Long and technical; the indication and effectiveness sections are the most readable.

10. [De Novo DEN150048: gammaCore Non-invasive Vagus Nerve Stimulator](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/denovo.cfm?id=DEN150048) - US Food and Drug Administration device database - The record of the April 14, 2017 De Novo authorization of gammaCore for cluster headache, a useful example of an FDA authorization rather than an approval.

11. [The inflammatory reflex](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12490958/) - Tracey KJ. *Nature*, 2002 - The influential review proposing that the nervous system reflexively controls inflammation, the idea behind vagus nerve stimulation for rheumatoid arthritis and the field of bioelectronic medicine.

12. [Amitriptyline at Low-Dose and Titrated for Irritable Bowel Syndrome as Second-Line Treatment in primary care (ATLANTIS)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37858323/) - Ford AC, et al. *The Lancet*, 2023 - The 463-person randomized trial analyzed in this chapter's "How to read a gut-brain claim" box, showing low-dose amitriptyline helped IBS symptoms more than placebo.
