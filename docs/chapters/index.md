# List of Chapters

Each chapter has a MicroSim, a fictional At the Bedside case, Hype Meter grades, Your Gut Check practice, 12 exercises, a 10-question quiz, and references. Chapters list their prerequisites at the top.

## Part I. Two Nervous Systems, One Body

- **Chapter 1: [The Brain in Your Belly (and Why That Phrase Is Misleading)](01-brain-in-your-belly/index.md)**: Meet the enteric nervous system, the neuron network in your gut wall that moves food on its own, and learn the Hype Meter for grading gut-brain claims. ([Quiz](01-brain-in-your-belly/quiz.md) · [References](01-brain-in-your-belly/references.md))

- **Chapter 2: [The Vagus Highway](02-vagus-highway/index.md)**: How the vagus nerve, spinal nerves, and gut hormone cells carry messages between gut and brainstem, and why most vagus traffic runs upward. ([Quiz](02-vagus-highway/quiz.md) · [References](02-vagus-highway/references.md))

- **Chapter 3: [Meet the Microbiome](03-meet-the-microbiome/index.md)**: Meet the trillions of microbes in your gut: how many there are, how they settle in at birth, how antibiotics shake them up, and how scientists study them. ([Quiz](03-meet-the-microbiome/quiz.md) · [References](03-meet-the-microbiome/references.md))

- **Chapter 4: [The Immune System as a Sense Organ](04-immune-sense-organ/index.md)**: How the gut's immune system senses microbes, sends cytokine messages to the brain, and creates the tired, low feeling of sickness behavior. ([Quiz](04-immune-sense-organ/quiz.md) · [References](04-immune-sense-organ/references.md))

## Part II. How the Signals Travel

- **Chapter 5: [Chemical Messengers and the Serotonin Myth](05-serotonin-myth/index.md)**: Most of your serotonin is made in your gut, but it never reaches your brain. Learn where serotonin goes, what SSRIs do, and what gut microbes really make. ([Quiz](05-serotonin-myth/quiz.md) · [References](05-serotonin-myth/references.md))

- **Chapter 6: [Fiber, Fatty Acids, and the Blood-Brain Barrier](06-fiber-fatty-acids-bbb/index.md)**: Follow fiber from your plate to the microbes in your colon, meet the short-chain fatty acids they make, and sort real gut-barrier science from leaky gut hype. ([Quiz](06-fiber-fatty-acids-bbb/quiz.md) · [References](06-fiber-fatty-acids-bbb/references.md))

- **Chapter 7: [Stress and the Gut](07-stress-and-the-gut/index.md)**: Learn how the HPA axis and fight-or-flight system turn stress into real changes in gut movement, sensitivity, and barrier function, and what germ-free mice reveal. ([Quiz](07-stress-and-the-gut/quiz.md) · [References](07-stress-and-the-gut/references.md))

- **Chapter 8: [Pain, Sensitivity, and the Gut](08-pain-and-sensitivity/index.md)**: Why the same gut stretch can hurt one person and not another: nociceptors, spinal gates, the brain's volume knob, and visceral hypersensitivity. ([Quiz](08-pain-and-sensitivity/quiz.md) · [References](08-pain-and-sensitivity/references.md))

## Part III. When the System Goes Wrong

- **Chapter 9: [Inflammatory Bowel Disease: Ulcerative Colitis and Crohn's](09-inflammatory-bowel-disease/index.md)**: How Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis work, how doctors find and treat the inflammation, and how IBD connects to energy, mood, and the brain. ([Quiz](09-inflammatory-bowel-disease/quiz.md) · [References](09-inflammatory-bowel-disease/references.md))

- **Chapter 10: [IBS and Disorders of Gut-Brain Interaction](10-ibs-and-dgbi/index.md)**: Why IBS is real even when tests are normal, how the Rome criteria work (Rome IV and the new Rome V), and which treatments have good evidence. ([Quiz](10-ibs-and-dgbi/quiz.md) · [References](10-ibs-and-dgbi/references.md))

- **Chapter 11: [Anxiety, Depression, and the Gut](11-anxiety-depression-gut/index.md)**: Why anxiety and depression are more common with gut conditions, how the link runs both ways, and how to tell correlation from cause. ([Quiz](11-anxiety-depression-gut/quiz.md) · [References](11-anxiety-depression-gut/references.md))

- **Chapter 12: [Celiac Disease and Food Reactions](12-celiac-and-food-reactions/index.md)**: How celiac disease flattens the villi of the small intestine, how doctors test for it, and how it differs from food allergy, intolerance, and gluten sensitivity. ([Quiz](12-celiac-and-food-reactions/quiz.md) · [References](12-celiac-and-food-reactions/references.md))

- **Chapter 13: [Autism, ADHD, and the Gut](13-autism-adhd-gut/index.md)**: What the evidence really says about autism, ADHD, and the gut: real GI symptoms worth treating, diet as a confounder, and why cure claims are hype. ([Quiz](13-autism-adhd-gut/quiz.md) · [References](13-autism-adhd-gut/references.md))

- **Chapter 14: [Parkinson's, Alzheimer's, and the Gut](14-parkinsons-alzheimers-gut/index.md)**: Why constipation can appear years before Parkinson's, what the gut-first and brain-first ideas predict, and what we really know about Alzheimer's and gut microbes. ([Quiz](14-parkinsons-alzheimers-gut/quiz.md) · [References](14-parkinsons-alzheimers-gut/references.md))
