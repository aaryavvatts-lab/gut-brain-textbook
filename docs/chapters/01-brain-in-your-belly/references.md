# References: The Brain in Your Belly (and Why That Phrase Is Misleading)

1. [23.1 Overview of the Digestive System](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-1-overview-of-the-digestive-system) - OpenStax, *Anatomy and Physiology 2e* (CC BY 4.0) - Free open textbook section on the layers of the gut wall, smooth muscle, and the gut's nerve supply. A good, readable place to review the anatomy behind Sections 1.2 to 1.4. (Note: it gives an older, lower estimate of about 100 million enteric neurons.)

2. [Hirschsprung Disease](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/hirschsprung-disease) - National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK) - The US government's plain-language guide to Hirschsprung disease, with linked pages on symptoms, diagnosis, and treatment. Source for the 1 in 5,000 figure, the types of Hirschsprung disease by segment length (its "Definition and Facts" page: about 80% short-segment, about 5% to 10% the whole large intestine), newborn warning signs, and the pull-through surgery described in the At the Bedside case.

3. [Hirschsprung Disease](https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/condition/hirschsprung-disease/) - MedlinePlus Genetics, US National Library of Medicine - Explains how often Hirschsprung disease occurs and which genes (including RET) are involved, in clear language for families.

4. [Enteric nervous system: sensory transduction, neural circuits and gastrointestinal motility](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7474470/) - Spencer NJ, Hu H. *Nature Reviews Gastroenterology & Hepatology*, 2020 - A research review that tells the history of isolated-gut experiments from 1755 onward, explains the peristaltic reflex, and reports that serotonin from the gut lining is not required for peristalsis. Advanced, but the introduction is worth reading.

5. [The Enteric Nervous System and Its Emerging Role as a Therapeutic Target](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7495222/) - Fleming MA, Ehsan L, Moore SR, Levin DE. *Gastroenterology Research and Practice*, 2020 - An open-access overview of ENS structure, neuron types, and glia. Source for the 400 to 600 million neuron estimate. (Its sentence on the cause of Hirschsprung disease contains an error; use the Heuckeroth review for that.)

6. [The bowel and beyond: the enteric nervous system in neurological disorders](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5005185/) - Rao M, Gershon MD. *Nature Reviews Gastroenterology & Hepatology*, 2016 - Review co-written by the author of *The Second Brain*; explains why the ENS is called the third division of the autonomic nervous system and compares its neuron count with the spinal cord.

7. [Enteric nervous system development: a crest cell's journey from neural tube to colon](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5474363/) - Nagy N, Goldstein AM. *Seminars in Cell & Developmental Biology*, 2017 - Describes how neural crest cells migrate into the gut and colonize it from top to bottom, and how failures of that journey lead to Hirschsprung disease.

8. [Hirschsprung disease: integrating basic science and clinical medicine to improve outcomes](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29300049/) - Heuckeroth RO. *Nature Reviews Gastroenterology & Hepatology*, 2018 - Review explaining why a bowel segment without enteric neurons stays contracted and blocks stool. The PubMed abstract is a clear one-paragraph summary.

9. [Mechanism of Interdigestive Migrating Motor Complex](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3400812/) - Takahashi T. *Journal of Neurogastroenterology and Motility*, 2012 - Open-access review of the migrating motor complex, the between-meal "housekeeping" contractions, including how often they recur in humans.

10. [The Human and Mouse Enteric Nervous System at Single-Cell Resolution](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32888429/) - Drokhlyansky E, et al. *Cell*, 2020 - The enteric neuron atlas analyzed in this chapter's "How to read a gut-brain claim" box. The abstract gives the cell counts; the full text lists funding and conflicts of interest.

11. [Novel Neural Cell Therapy for Hirschsprung Disease and Related Enteric Nervous System Disorders](https://2025.worldmedicalinnovation.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/WMIF-2025-First-Look-Abstract-Goldstein.pdf) - Goldstein A. World Medical Innovation Forum abstract, Mass General Brigham, 2025 - A short research summary: mouse studies of patient-derived enteric neural stem cells largely complete, an FDA application (IND) planned before a first human trial. Cited in the At the Bedside case; this is a status update, not a published trial.

12. [Chapter 1: Neurons, Glia, and the Nervous System](https://aaryavvatts-lab.github.io/neuroscience-textbook/chapters/01-neurons-and-glia/) - *From Neuron to Bedside* (book #1 in this series) - A free refresher on neurons, glia, and the central and peripheral nervous systems, if you want more background before Section 1.3.
