# Roadmap

## Version 1.0 (October 2026)

- 20 chapters in 5 parts, each with a MicroSim, a fictional case, Hype Meter grades, Your Gut Check practice, 12 exercises, and a 10-question quiz
- A learning graph of 337 concepts, a glossary, an FAQ, and 7 appendices
- Printable PDFs, a teacher guide, a club kit, and a pilot study kit

## Planned next

- **Expert review.** Invite gastroenterologists, psychiatrists, researchers, and patient advocates to review chapters, and publish their corrections on the [Accuracy](accuracy.md) page.
- **Reader review of Chapter 13.** Ask autistic and ADHD readers to review the chapter on autism, ADHD, and the gut, and revise based on what they say.
- **A small pilot study.** Use the [Pilot Study Kit](appendices/pilot-study-kit.md) with a school club to measure what readers learn.
- **Evidence updates.** Gut-brain science changes fast. Recheck every Hype Meter grade, statistic, and approval at least once a year.
- **Translations and audio.** Explore translated chapters and read-aloud versions, starting with the family and stigma chapters.
