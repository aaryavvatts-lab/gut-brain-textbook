# Gut Check: The Neuroscience of How Your Gut and Brain Talk

![Gut Check: The Neuroscience of How Your Gut and Brain Talk. What is real, what is hype.](img/cover.png){ width="100%" }

**A free, interactive neuroscience textbook for teens, patients, and families. What is real, what is hype.**

Your gut has its own network of hundreds of millions of neurons, trillions of microbes, and a large share of your body's antibody-making cells, and all of them send signals to your brain. That conversation helps explain why stress can upset your stomach, why gut diseases so often travel with anxiety and depression, and why so many "heal your gut, fix your mind" posts sound convincing. Among adults with inflammatory bowel disease, about one in three report anxiety symptoms and about one in four report depression symptoms.[^barberio] In 2025, about one in three US high school students (33.3%) reported persistent feelings of sadness or hopelessness.[^yrbs] This book teaches the real neuroscience behind the gut-brain connection, and it grades every popular claim for how strong the evidence actually is.

## What makes this book different

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-gauge:{ .lg .middle } **The Hype Meter**

    ---

    Every popular claim gets a visible grade: **Strong**, **Moderate**, **Preliminary**, or **Hype**. You'll always know whether a finding comes from mice or people, and whether it shows correlation or cause.

-   :material-gesture-tap:{ .lg .middle } **Hands-on simulations**

## Part of a series

*Gut Check* is the second book by the same author. The first, [*From Neuron to Bedside*](https://aaryavvatts-lab.github.io/neuroscience-textbook/), teaches how the brain works. This one teaches how the brain is wired to the rest of the body. You don't need to read the first book, but chapters link to it when a refresher helps.
